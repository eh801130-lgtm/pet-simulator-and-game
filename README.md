# Pet Paradise 🐾

A cozy, browser-based pet simulator with a large walk-around world, care actions, pet customization, and four mini-games.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build tools or dependencies are required. You can also use GitHub Pages to publish the repository as a website: open **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save. GitHub will show the published URL on that page after deployment.

## Features

- Click in the world or use arrow keys/WASD to move your pet.
- Feed, play with, cuddle, and clean your companion; needs gradually change over time.
- Change the pet's name, creature style, coat color, accessory, or upload your own image.
- Click your pet for changing, need-aware messages.
- Play Pac-Man, Pong, Tic-Tac-Toe, and two-player Chess.

The chess mini-game demonstrates piece movement and captures, but does not implement full tournament rules such as castling or check validation.
